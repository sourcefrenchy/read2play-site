Why kids love it

And why parents keep it on the home screen.

Read to earn

Kids bank reading time and spend it on screen time, at a rate each parent sets per child.

🐉

148+ creatures

Reading milestones unlock trading-card creatures. Kids at the same level get different cards, so every collection is their own.

⚔️

Real battles

Five rounds of choices: pick a creature, then Strike, Guard or a Special. Underdog Spirit keeps younger siblings in the game.

🧮

Book challenges

Finishing a book earns a Book Rare card and a quick math challenge matched to your child's age.

🏆

Optional leaderboard

Off by default. A parent can turn it on to compare reading with families everywhere, using a nickname only.

🔒

Private by design

No accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. Data stays on your device and your own iCloud.

How it works

Set up in a minute, no account needed.

1

Parent sets up

Create a PIN, add your kids and choose how many play minutes each reading minute earns.

2

Kids tap Read

Reading time fills their bank and unlocks creature cards along the way.

3

Kids tap Play

They spend what they earned, with a friendly chime when time is up.
