Real battles

Brain Boost

Reading this week, minutes per day, streaks and books finished, all on one screen.

How it works

Set up in a minute, no account needed.

1

Parent sets up

Create a PIN, add your kids and set each one's rule, like 15 min reading = 10 min play.

2

Kids tap Read

Reading time fills their bank and unlocks creature cards along the way.

3

Kids tap Play

They spend what they earned, with a friendly chime when time is up.
